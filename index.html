What is inside

A studio, a queue and a safety net.

Eight screens cover the whole weekly routine: create the content, decide when it goes out, keep an eye on the connectors, and talk to your audience without breaking the rules.

Content studio

Pick channels, a content pillar and a brief. Get on-brand copy, hashtags, a CTA and a locally rendered card. Eight pillars, multiple variants, editable before anything is queued.

Queue and scheduler

Everything sits in one queue with its status, time, retries and publish controls. Guard rails hold anything that would exceed a cap or leave the sending window.

Recurring cadences

Say “Mon/Wed/Fri at 09:00, a tip, Facebook and Instagram” once. The scheduler writes, renders and either drafts or approves each batch. Auto-approve is optional.

Audience and outreach

Import a CSV of your own contacts, check each lead, draft personalised email or WhatsApp messages and approve them one by one. Suppression is checked before every send.

A brand per business

Keep several brands in one workspace with separate profiles, colours, sending windows and guard rails. Switch the whole app from the top bar.

Activity log

Every publish, send, hold and failure is recorded with the API response in plain language. When a token expires, the log tells you which one and why.

The safety model

Aggressive automation. Unusually cautious defaults.

Marketing tools tend to assume you want maximum reach immediately. MarketPilot assumes the opposite: you want to read the output first, and you want a clear record of what left the machine.

  • Dry run is ON at first launch. Posts and messages are marked SIMULATED and nothing is uploaded.
  • Approval lock is ON, so auto-approving cadences still wait for a human.
  • Sending window, daily cap per channel and minimum gap are enforced by the scheduler.
  • Retries only for rate limits and 5xx errors. Authentication failures stop immediately.
  • One contact per campaign, a permanent suppression list, and a lawful-basis confirmation before outreach.

Common questions

Before you flip the switch.

Will it post anything without my approval?

No, not on a fresh install. Dry run is on, so publishes and sends are simulated and marked SIMULATED. The approval lock is also on, so cadence auto-approve is ignored until you turn it off. You can loosen both, but you have to do it deliberately.

Do I need API keys on day one?

No. Without an AI key the app uses deterministic templates, and the Manual channel needs no credentials at all. Add a Gemini, OpenAI or OpenCode key when you want copy tailored to each brand and contact.

Can I use it for my customers too?

Yes. Add a brand per business in the same workspace, or install a second copy under another Windows user. Channel credentials are stored per installation, not per brand, so unrelated customers should not share one install.

Does it scrape or use purchased lists?

No. Every connector uses an official API, and every outreach campaign requires you to confirm a lawful basis. There are no drip sequences and no automatic follow-ups — one contact per campaign, by design.
