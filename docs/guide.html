Setup guide, A to Z

A practical walkthrough from a blank install to a working autopilot. Follow it in order the first time; after that, the daily routine at the end is all you need.

Budget about 45 minutes for the first pass. You will install the app, describe one business properly, connect one channel, and watch a simulated post travel from a brief to the queue. Nothing will reach an audience until you decide it should.

0. Install and open

Installer (recommended). Run MarketPilot-1.1.0.msi, approve the SmartScreen prompt if Windows shows one, then launch MarketPilot from the Start menu. The install is per user and does not need administrator rights.

Portable. Extract the ZIP, keep the folder intact, and run MarketPilot\MarketPilot.exe. The runtime is inside that folder; moving the EXE alone will not work.

On first launch the app creates %APPDATA%\MarketPilot with one brand called “My Business”, dry run ON, the scheduler ON, and the approval lock ON. It also opens the Dashboard.

No account, no onboarding wizard. The app does not ask you to sign up or upload anything. The first useful step is the brand profile, not a tour.

Trial and licence

Every installation starts with a 72-hour trial of the whole app: no card, no account, no feature gates. The clock starts on first launch, not on download. The start time is mirrored in the data folder, so deleting one file does not hand out a fresh trial.

After the trial, order a term from License & plans in the sidebar: 1 month for $15, 3 months for $39 or 1 year for $129 in USD. Each plan has a Buy on WhatsApp button; the message is pre-filled with the plan and price. The key arrives in the same chat. Paste it into License & plans and press Activate. The signature is checked on your PC, so activation works offline and needs no account.

A key is accepted once per installation and adds its full term to the end date, measured from whichever is later: today, or the day your current term ends. Renewing early never shortens anything. The key id is shown on the same screen; quote it when you need a replacement or a renewal.

When a term ends the app becomes read-only. Brands, drafts, posts, leads, campaigns and settings stay visible and stay on disk. Creating, approving, publishing, importing contacts and outreach pause, and the scheduler stops sending. Nothing is deleted. Activate a key to continue exactly where you left off.

From a terminal, MarketPilot-cli.cmd --license prints the state and --activate <key> activates without opening the window. The CLI exits with code 2 while the app is read-only, so a scheduled task fails loudly instead of silently doing nothing. If the Windows clock jumps backwards by more than two hours, the app pauses with a “clock moved backwards” note until the date and time are correct again.

1. Learn the layout

The left sidebar is the whole app. The top bar holds the current brand, the dry-run switch, Run tick, and Approve all drafts. Toasts appear at the bottom right and every error also lands in the Activity log.

Two rules of the house:

  1. Dry run ON means everything is simulated and marked SIMULATED. Nothing is posted, emailed, messaged, or written to the outbox.
  2. Approval lock ON means even auto-approving cadences wait for you.

Leave both alone until you have read the output and trust it.

2. Fill the brand profile

Open Brand profile. This is the most important step: the writer treats these fields as facts and will not invent what is missing.

Identity and offer

  • Business name, industry, city or area, time zone, website, booking link, phone. The copy uses these literally.
  • Services and proof — one item per line: services, what is genuinely different, current offers. Be specific: “emergency call-outs within 60 minutes in Cluj” beats “quality service”.
  • Words your audience uses / never use — keywords sharpen the voice; banned words are enforced in the prompt.
  • Voice — tone in plain words, for example “Warm, direct, no hype. Short sentences. Never talk down to the reader.”

Look and sending identity

  • Brand and accent colour — used by the local artwork renderer for every card.
  • From name, reply-to, mailing address, unsubscribe URL, signature — required before real email outreach. Most countries require a physical address and a working opt-out in commercial email.

Guard rails

  • Sending window — default 08:00–20:00 in the brand timezone. Nothing is published or sent outside it.
  • Daily cap per channel — the scheduler holds anything over the cap.
  • Minimum gap between posts — prevents a burst if several posts are due at once.

Press Save profile. The Dashboard keeps a “Finish the brand profile first” panel until name, one-liner and audience are filled.

Several businesses

Click New brand and repeat. The brand selector in the top bar switches the whole app. Channel credentials are stored per installation, not per brand, so two brands on one install share the same Page, token and mailbox. For two unrelated customers, use two Windows users or two installs.

3. Connect the AI writer (optional)

Open Settings → AI writer.

  1. Pick a provider

    Gemini (the free tier is enough), OpenAI, OpenCode Zen or OpenCode Go.

  2. Keep the preselected model or type one

    Any model available to your key works; the app sends a strict-JSON request.

  3. Paste the matching key and save

    Then press Test key & model. Errors are shown in plain language rather than a raw stack trace.

Without a key, everything still runs on deterministic templates. With a key, the copy is tailored to the brand, the pillar and each lead. The key is stored in settings.properties as plain text on your Windows account, so treat that account as the security boundary.

What leaves the machine. When you generate, the brand profile, brief and selected channel limits are sent to the AI provider you configured. Artwork is rendered locally. Without a key, no content leaves the machine at all.

5. Write your first post

Open Content studio.

  1. Channels

    Click one or more, for example Facebook + Instagram + Manual.

  2. Content pillar

    Choose the angle. Tip is the safest first post.

  3. Variants per channel

    1 is fine; 2–3 gives you options.

  4. Brief / topic

    Optional, but the more concrete the better: “explain why a slow website loses bookings, with a before/after”.

  5. Generate drafts

    Each draft shows the copy, hashtags, CTA, character count against the channel limit, and the generated branded card.

The eight pillars

PillarWhat the writer aims for
TipTeach one useful thing the audience can apply today. Proof comes from the craft, not claims.
ProofA concrete before/after, process detail or honest customer outcome. Never an invented testimonial.
OfferThe current offer clearly: who it is for, what they get, what to do next. No fake urgency.
StoryA short human story from running the business, ending with a lesson the reader can use.
FAQOne question customers actually ask, answered in their words.
BehindHow the work is done, step by step, so the reader trusts the process.
LocalTies the post to the town or neighbourhood and the people who live there.
MythCorrects a common misconception with a calm, factual explanation.

Edit any field in place: internal label, post text, hashtags, link, and the public image URL (required for Instagram, optional elsewhere). Then choose:

  • Save — keeps it as a draft.
  • Approve & queue — approves it at the time in Schedule. Leave the field empty to queue it for now, or press Use next hour.
  • Publish now — same as approving for the next tick; guard rails still apply.
  • Re-render art — rebuilds the PNG after changing text or colours.

Nothing goes out yet. The post sits in Queue as APPROVED.

6. Publish it, safely

Open Queue. Filters: All, Draft, Queued, Published, Dry run, Failed. Each row has the controls it needs: approve, publish now, cancel, re-queue or open the image folder.

With dry run ON, the next scheduler tick — within 30 seconds — marks the post SIMULATED and logs it. You can also press Run tick in the top bar.

When you are ready for a real post:

  1. Switch the chip from Dry run ON to Live

    It is in the top bar and affects publishing and outreach.

  2. Approve one post to a low-risk channel

    A Facebook Page is ideal for the first live test.

  3. Run a tick or wait 30 seconds

    Then check the Activity log for the API response and the Queue row for PUBLISHED and a working link.

  4. Turn dry run back ON

    Keep it on until you trust the cadence and the voice.

FAILED rows show the reason. Rate limits retry after 15 and then 30 minutes; authentication errors stop immediately and need a new token.

7. Automate with cadences

Open Automation → New cadence. A cadence is a standing instruction, not a piece of content.

  • Name — “Monday tips”.
  • Channels — the mix for this cadence.
  • Days — Mon–Sun toggles.
  • Time (HH:mm) — in the brand timezone.
  • Pillar and brief — what kind of content it writes.
  • Enabled — on or off without deleting it.
  • Auto-approve generated posts — posts go straight to the queue.

Every day at the chosen time, the scheduler writes one post per channel, renders the artwork, and either drafts or approves it. Run now generates a batch immediately without changing the schedule.

With the approval lock ON (Settings → Safety), auto-approve is ignored and every cadence post waits in Queue. That is the recommended setting for the first weeks.

A cadence fires once per day. If no post appears, check: enabled, weekday, time already passed, approval lock, and the Activity log. The automation chapter has the full scheduling model.

8. Manual bundles for TikTok, Reels, Stories

TikTok has no self-serve posting API for most accounts, so Manual is the honest path.

  1. Use the Manual channel

    In Studio or a cadence, choose Manual / TikTok / Reels.

  2. Generate, edit, approve and queue

    Exactly like any other post.

  3. Turn dry run OFF

    Dry run does not write bundles. This is the one action that needs live mode even though nothing is uploaded.

  4. Run a tick

    Open %APPDATA%\MarketPilot\outbox\<brand>\<post>\, also reachable from Settings → Open outbox folder.

The bundle contains caption.txt, image.png, post.json, README.txt, and reel.mp4 when FFmpeg is available. Upload from your phone, paste the caption and hashtags, done.

For reels: install FFmpeg, set its full path in Settings → Short-form video, press Check, and keep Render vertical reels on. The app builds an 8-second 1080×1920 clip from the branded card with a slow pan and zoom. No stock footage, no API cost.

9. Add contacts

Open Audience → Import CSV or Add lead. A header row is easiest and the columns can be in any order:

company,name,email,phone,city,category,notes,source
Northline Plumbing,Ana Pop,ana@northline.ro,+40740111222,Cluj,plumbing,met at expo,expo

Accepted headers: company, name, email, phone, city, category, notes, source. Without a header, the order is company, contact, email, phone, city. Rows are deduplicated on email or phone.

For each lead:

  • Subscribed to marketing — required for newsletters. One-to-one outreach does not require it but still needs the campaign confirmation.
  • Unsubscribe — adds the address to the permanent suppression list.

10. Run a cold-outreach campaign

Open Audience → New campaign.

  1. Type and channel

    One-to-one outreach, by Email or WhatsApp.

  2. Daily cap and goal

    How many messages this campaign may send per day, and one sentence such as “book 5 site audits”.

  3. Subject and body templates

    Use {{name}}, {{company}}, {{city}}, {{topic}} and {{business}}. With an AI key these are rewritten per contact; without one the placeholders are filled literally.

  4. Auto-approve personalised messages

    Off means every message is reviewed. Keep it off while you are learning the voice.

  5. Save campaign

Then:

  1. Tick “I have a lawful basis to contact these contacts”

    Without it the campaign cannot be prepared or sent. This is your legal responsibility, not a formality.

  2. Activate

    The campaign is now eligible for drafting and sending.

  3. Draft 10 messages

    The engine personalises up to 10 contacts that are not suppressed, not unsubscribed and not already in this campaign.

  4. Review every message

    Approve, skip, re-queue or delete it in the Messages panel.

  5. Send approved

    Or let the scheduler send inside the brand's sending window, within the daily cap.

Guards that always apply: one message per contact per campaign, the suppression list, an unsubscribe footer and header on email, quiet hours, the daily cap, and dry run (sends become SIMULATED). When someone asks to stop, press Unsubscribe on the lead; that suppresses them forever.

Cold email rules differ by country. Legitimate interest, B2B-only rules and opt-in requirements vary. MarketPilot records your confirmation and enforces the mechanics; it cannot make the contact lawful. Use your own customers first, keep a real sender identity, and honour opt-outs immediately.

11. Send a newsletter

Same flow as outreach, with two differences:

  • Type — Newsletter.
  • Recipients — only contacts marked Subscribed.

It goes to email only. Drafts are personalised per subscriber, reviewed the same way, and sent through SMTP. Keep the mailing address and unsubscribe URL in the brand profile accurate; most countries require both.

12. Local and Google Business posts

Connect Google Business Profile in Settings, then choose that channel in Studio or a cadence. The Local pillar ties copy to the city or neighbourhood. These are short updates and offers, not blog articles, and the image is sized 1200×900 locally.

13. Your daily and weekly routine

Daily — 5 minutes

  1. Dashboard: scheduler running, failures, next cadence.
  2. Queue: review cadence drafts, approve or edit.
  3. Audience: approve outreach drafted overnight.
  4. Activity log: read any WARN or ERROR and fix tokens or content.

Weekly — 20 minutes

  1. Generate one Studio batch with a strong brief.
  2. Check reach and engagement on the platforms; adjust pillars.
  3. Export a Manual bundle for TikTok or Reels.
  4. Review the suppression list and unsubscribe replies.
  5. Update offers in the brand profile so future copy stays true.
