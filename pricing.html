How buying works

Four steps, one chat.

1 · Message us

Tap Buy on WhatsApp under a plan. The message is pre-filled with the plan and price, so there is nothing to type.

2 · Pay and receive a key

We confirm the amount and the payment method, then send a licence key in the same chat. No card details are entered into the app.

3 · Activate offline

Open License & plans, paste the key and press Activate. The signature is checked on your PC; no account or server call is involved.

4 · Renew before the date

Message us again for a renewal key. Its full term is added to your end date, so renewing early never shortens anything.

Licence questions

The fine print, in plain words.

What exactly happens after the 72 hours?

The app switches to read-only. Your brands, drafts, posts, leads, campaigns and settings stay visible and stay on disk. Generating copy, approving, publishing, importing contacts and outreach pause, and the scheduler stops sending. Activate a key to pick up exactly where you left off.

Is the trial the full app?

Yes. Every channel, the studio, artwork, reels, cadences, outreach, newsletters and the CLI are unlocked for 72 hours from first launch. There is no separate trial build and no card is collected.

What does a licence key look like?

Four short segments and a signature, for example MP1.YEAR.K7Q2M9XD.2026-11-15.…. Paste the whole thing. The app verifies the signature offline, so a mistyped or invented key is rejected immediately.

Can I move the licence to a new PC?

One key activates once, on one installation. If you replace your PC or reinstall Windows, message us with the key id shown under License & plans and we re-issue it. Keys are not tied to a hardware fingerprint, so a normal reinstall keeps your remaining days in the data folder.

Do prices include tax?

Prices are shown in USD before any local tax or transfer fee. We confirm the final amount in the WhatsApp chat before you pay.

What if I buy the wrong plan?

If the key has not been activated, message us within seven days and we swap it for the plan you meant. Activated keys are not refundable, but the remaining term is never lost when you renew early.

Is there a subscription that charges me automatically?

No. These are prepaid terms. Nothing is stored, no card is kept on file and no payment happens inside the app. You decide when to renew.

Does the app phone home?

The licence check is local. The app contacts the internet only for the services you configure: your AI provider, and the social, email or WhatsApp APIs you connect. It sends no telemetry.
